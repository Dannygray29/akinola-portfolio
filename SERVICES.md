## How engagements work

1. Send the repository or a clear description of the problem by email.
2. I review the scope and reply with a written deliverable, estimate, and timeline.
3. Work is carried out in a branch or pull request where possible.
4. You review the changes and agree acceptance before completion.

Small, clearly bounded tasks are preferred. Pricing is quoted after reviewing the actual scope; no fixed price or delivery time is promised before inspection.

## Working principles

- AI assistance is used in research, implementation, and debugging; it is disclosed rather than represented as unaided human work.
- No claim of formal employment history or professional certification is made.
- No production deployment, destructive change, or handling of client secrets without explicit authorization.
- Tests and limitations are reported honestly. A passing build is not presented as proof of every runtime behaviour.
- Third-party code and dependencies retain their original licences and attribution.
